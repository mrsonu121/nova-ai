What is NOVA? +

NOVA is an AI-powered productivity platform that brings your projects, tasks, docs, and team conversations into one calm, connected workspace.

Can I try NOVA for free? +

Absolutely. The Solo plan is free forever, and every Studio workspace starts with a 14-day free trial. No credit card required.

How does the AI assistant work? +

NOVA's assistant uses the context you choose to share to summarize, prioritize, draft, and turn ideas into clear next steps.

Can I invite people outside my team? +

Yes. Studio and Scale plans include flexible guest access so clients and collaborators can join the right projects without extra friction.

Is my team's data secure? +

Your workspace is private by default. NOVA uses encryption in transit and at rest, role-based permissions, and regular security reviews.
